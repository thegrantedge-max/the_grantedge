This Is Not a Sales Call. It Is a Professional Strategy Conversation Built Around Your Mission.

The GRANTEdge Free Funding Strategy Call is a focused, high-value, no-obligation conversation between our team and your organizational leadership designed to give you real insight, honest assessment, and concrete direction regardless of whether you choose to work with us afterward.

In this call, we will take the time to understand your mission, your programs, your current funding situation, and your growth goals. You will leave this call with clarity and direction. No pressure. No obligation. Just a real conversation between professionals about an important mission and what it will take to fund it sustainably.

Here Is Exactly What Happens Before, During, and After Your Free Strategy Call.

Before

After you complete the booking form, you will receive a confirmation and a brief pre-call questionnaire. Taking five to ten minutes to complete it thoughtfully allows our team to prepare meaningfully for the conversation.

During

We listen first. We ask questions designed to give us a complete picture of where your organization is and where you want to take it. We share honest assessments and specific recommendations based on what you tell us.

After

If there is a strong fit, we will follow up with a tailored proposal outlining the specific services we recommend. If GRANTEdge is simply not the right fit, we will tell you that honestly and point you toward resources that might serve you better.

Frequently Asked Questions About Working With GRANTEdge

What happens if my organization is not grant ready yet?

That is exactly the kind of situation our free strategy call is designed to address. We will assess where you stand honestly, identify the specific gaps that need to be addressed, and give you a clear roadmap for getting ready. Many organizations come to GRANTEdge precisely because they need help getting to a point where they can compete effectively for funding. Grant readiness support is one of our core services.

Do you only work with certain types of nonprofits or certain states?

GRANTEdge works with nonprofits, faith-based organizations, grassroots organizations, and social enterprises of every size and structure. We serve organizations nationally and our services are not limited to any specific geographic area.

How long does it typically take to see results from working with GRANTEdge?

This depends significantly on where your organization is starting from. Organizations that are already grant-ready and have a strong program to fund can see applications submitted within the first thirty to sixty days of an engagement. Organizations that need foundational readiness work first may take longer to reach the submission stage, but that investment in readiness dramatically improves results once applications do go out.

Do you guarantee that we will receive grant funding?

No ethical funding strategy partner makes that guarantee, and we will not either. What we guarantee is that every element of your funding strategy, from funder research to proposal quality to organizational readiness to digital visibility, will be developed and executed at the highest professional standard, giving your organization the strongest possible competitive position in the funding landscape.

Will you work alongside our existing development staff or board?

Absolutely. GRANTEdge is designed to complement and strengthen whatever internal capacity your organization already has. We work collaboratively with development staff, executive directors, and board members to integrate our expertise with your team's knowledge of your mission and community.

What information do you need to get started?

We begin every engagement with our strategic discovery process, which gives us everything we need to build your customized funding strategy. The pre-call questionnaire is the first step, followed by a deeper discovery conversation at the start of your engagement. You do not need to have everything organized before reaching out. That is part of what we help you build.

Do you share pricing on your website?

We do not list pricing publicly because every engagement is customized to the specific needs, goals, and capacity of each organization we serve. During or after your free strategy call, we will share a tailored proposal that reflects exactly what your organization needs and what the investment looks like.

How is GRANTEdge different from hiring a freelance grant writer?

A freelance grant writer handles proposals. GRANTEdge handles the entire funding infrastructure. That means funder research, proposal writing, grant readiness and compliance support, fundraising strategy, donor engagement, nonprofit marketing, digital visibility, and Google Ad Grants management, all coordinated by a specialized team working toward the same goal for your organization.
