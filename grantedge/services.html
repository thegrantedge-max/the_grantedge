Grant Research and Prospecting

Find the Right Funders. Apply With Confidence. Stop Wasting Time on the Wrong Opportunities.

The foundation of any successful grant strategy is knowing exactly who to ask. This sounds simple. In practice, it requires significant research, sector knowledge, analytical skill, and an understanding of the philanthropic landscape that most organizations simply do not have the time or internal expertise to develop.

Harriet Edward leads GRANTEdge's grant research and prospecting work, bringing a rigorous, systematic approach to funder identification and qualification that dramatically improves the strategic alignment of every application our clients submit.

Our research process examines foundation giving patterns including which organizations they have funded historically, at what funding levels, for which types of programs, and in which geographic areas. We analyze government grant priorities, corporate foundation interests, and community foundation focus areas. We evaluate eligibility requirements, application deadlines, funding cycles, and relationship history to build a prospect list that is genuinely aligned with your mission and ready to be pursued.

We also provide detailed funder profiles for every prioritized prospect, giving your team the intelligence you need to understand each funder's priorities, preferences, and application expectations before you submit a single word.

The difference between organizations that win grants at high rates and organizations that struggle comes down, more often than most people realize, to the quality and precision of their funder research. Organizations that apply broadly, chasing any available funding regardless of alignment, waste enormous time and resources and produce consistently disappointing results. Organizations that research strategically, building prospect lists that reflect genuine alignment between funder priorities and organizational programs, spend less time applying and win more often.

What Is Included

  • Systematic funder identification across foundations, government sources, and corporate giving programs
  • Detailed eligibility and alignment analysis for each prospect
  • Prioritized prospect list with strategic rationale for each recommendation
  • Individual funder profiles with giving history, priorities, and application guidance
  • Ongoing prospect pipeline management and updates as new opportunities emerge
  • Funder alignment scoring to help your team prioritize application efforts
  • Multi-year funding landscape analysis and opportunity mapping

Who This Is For

  • Organizations with low grant win rates who need to understand why their applications are not succeeding
  • Organizations entering new program areas or new geographic markets and need to identify aligned funders
  • Organizations building their first formal grant prospect pipeline
  • Organizations that want to move from reactive, opportunistic grant seeking to strategic, data-informed funder targeting

Grant Writing

Proposals That Speak the Language of Funders, Tell the Story of Your Mission, and Win.

A grant proposal is the most important strategic communication your organization produces. It is the document that stands between your mission and the funding it needs to grow. And yet most organizations treat it as an administrative task rather than the sophisticated, strategic communication challenge it actually is.

Kevin George leads GRANTEdge's grant writing practice with a depth of expertise and a commitment to quality that reflects the importance of this work. Kevin approaches every proposal as a custom-built strategic document designed to accomplish one specific goal: convincing a specific funder that your organization is the right investment for their resources.

That means every proposal we write is built from the ground up to align with the specific funder receiving it. We study their priorities, their language, their funded portfolio, and their evaluation criteria. We structure your narrative to address their specific questions and concerns. We present your outcomes, your theory of change, your community need evidence, and your organizational credibility in the format and language that this particular funder will find most compelling.

We write across all grant types including private foundation grants, community foundation grants, corporate foundation grants, federal grants, state grants, and capacity building grants. We work with organizations at every stage from their first grant application to complex multi-year federal funding proposals.

Grant proposal writing is one of the most demanding forms of professional writing precisely because it must accomplish so many objectives simultaneously. It must tell a compelling human story that makes funders feel the urgency and importance of your work. It must present clear, measurable program outcomes that demonstrate you understand what success looks like. It must provide evidence that the need you are addressing is real and significant. It must position your organization as uniquely qualified to address that need. And it must meet every technical requirement in the funder's guidelines with precision.

What Is Included

  • Narrative strategy and messaging framework development tailored to each funder
  • Budget narrative development in coordination with your finance team
  • Letter of inquiry development for funders who require pre-application submissions
  • Review, editing, and strengthening of existing proposals you have already drafted
  • Proposal template development for repeat funder applications
  • Case for support and organizational narrative development
  • Impact reports and funder stewardship communications

Who This Is For

  • Organizations that are struggling to write compelling proposals internally
  • Organizations with strong missions but weak proposal win rates
  • Organizations applying for their first major grant and wanting professional support

Grant Readiness Assessment

Before You Apply, Know Exactly Where You Stand and What Funders Will Find When They Look.

Grant readiness is one of the most consistently overlooked dimensions of nonprofit funding strategy. Organizations invest enormous time and energy in writing proposals without first ensuring that the organizational infrastructure those proposals are promising to funders actually exists and meets funder expectations.

The result is predictable. Proposals reach the finalist stage and then fail due diligence. Applications are declined not because the mission was weak but because the financials were not audit-ready, the board was not sufficiently engaged, the outcome measurement systems were not documented, or the compliance infrastructure had gaps that raised funder concern.

Rachel Bennett leads GRANTEdge's grant readiness assessment practice, bringing rigorous expertise to the systematic evaluation of organizational readiness across every dimension that funders evaluate.

Our assessment covers ten critical areas including financial management and documentation, audit and review readiness, board composition and engagement, program design and theory of change documentation, outcome measurement and evaluation systems, compliance with legal and regulatory requirements, human resources and personnel policies, communications and marketing credibility, technology and data management systems, and strategic planning documentation.

For each area, we provide a detailed assessment of your current status, a clear identification of gaps that could concern funders, and a prioritized roadmap for addressing those gaps in the sequence that will have the greatest impact on your funding competitiveness.

One of the most common and most costly mistakes nonprofits make is applying for significant funding before they are organizationally ready to receive and manage it. Funders conduct due diligence. They look beyond the proposal to the organization behind it. When those organizational elements are weak or absent, even the most compelling proposals fail.

What Is Included

  • Comprehensive organizational readiness evaluation across ten critical areas
  • Detailed written assessment report with findings and specific recommendations
  • Prioritized gap-closing roadmap with specific, actionable steps sequenced for maximum impact
  • Follow-up consultation to review findings and answer all questions
  • Optional ongoing support to implement recommended improvements
  • Board readiness and governance evaluation
  • Financial documentation and compliance review

Who This Is For

  • Organizations that have experienced repeated grant rejections without clear explanation
  • Organizations preparing to apply for their largest grant opportunity to date
  • Organizations entering a new stage of growth and wanting to ensure their infrastructure matches their ambition
  • Organizations that have never had a formal readiness assessment conducted
  • Organizations that want to understand exactly what funders will find when they look behind the proposal

Never Miss a Deadline. Never Leave a Requirement Unmet. Never Lose an Opportunity to Poor Process.

Grant application management is the operational backbone of a high-performing development function. It encompasses every step of the application process from initial opportunity identification through final submission and post-submission follow-up. When this function is managed poorly, organizations miss deadlines, submit incomplete applications, fail to meet technical requirements, and lose opportunities that their mission absolutely deserved to win.

GRANTEdge provides comprehensive grant application management that ensures every opportunity in your pipeline is pursued with maximum professionalism, complete accuracy, and perfect timeliness.

We begin by creating a detailed application calendar that maps every deadline, every submission requirement, every budget attachment, every supporting document, and every internal review step across your entire grant pipeline. We then coordinate the preparation of every element of each application, working with your team to gather the information, documentation, and data required, and managing the review and submission process from start to finish.

After submission, we monitor the status of pending applications, maintain communication with program officers where appropriate, prepare for site visits or follow-up questions when requested, and coordinate reporting requirements for funded grants.

The operational complexity of managing multiple grant applications simultaneously is one of the primary reasons organizations miss deadlines, submit incomplete packages, or fail to follow up appropriately after submission. Without a dedicated, systematic process for managing the application lifecycle, even well-written proposals can be undermined by process failures.

Fundraising Strategy

Grants Alone Are Not a Funding Strategy. Let Us Build One That Actually Sustains Your Mission.

One of the most common and most dangerous misconceptions in the nonprofit sector is that a grant strategy is the same thing as a fundraising strategy. It is not. Grants are one revenue source among many, and organizations that rely on grants as their primary or sole funding mechanism are chronically vulnerable to the volatility, unpredictability, and competitive pressure of the grant marketplace.

Sustainable nonprofit funding requires diversification. It requires a thoughtful, strategic plan that integrates multiple revenue streams, each with its own cultivation strategy, its own donor or funder relationships, its own communication systems, and its own management infrastructure, into a cohesive whole that produces stable, growing revenue over time.

GRANTEdge develops comprehensive fundraising strategies that build exactly this kind of diversified revenue base for the organizations we serve. Working in close coordination across our team's expertise in development strategy and research capabilities, we design fundraising plans that are realistic, sequenced appropriately for your organization's stage and capacity, and built around your specific mission, community, and relationships.

Our fundraising strategies address individual giving programs, major gifts cultivation, corporate partnership development, earned revenue opportunities, special events strategy, peer-to-peer fundraising, planned giving foundations, and the integration of all these streams with your grant strategy into a single, coordinated funding plan.

When grant funding is delayed, denied, or discontinued, organizations built entirely on grant revenue are immediately vulnerable. A properly diversified funding strategy ensures that no single source of revenue represents an existential risk to your organization's ability to deliver on its mission.

What Is Included

  • Comprehensive fundraising strategy development customized to your organization's stage and goals
  • Revenue diversification analysis and opportunity identification
  • Individual giving program design and implementation planning
  • Major gifts strategy and prospect identification
  • Corporate partnership development strategy
  • Earned revenue feasibility analysis
  • Annual fundraising calendar and implementation timeline
  • Staff and board engagement planning for fundraising activities

Who This Is For

  • Organizations that are over-reliant on grant funding and want to reduce that vulnerability
  • Organizations that have individual donors but no structured program for cultivating and retaining them
  • Organizations that are ready to launch a major gifts program
  • Organizations that want to build a comprehensive, long-term fundraising operation

Capacity Building

Funders Do Not Just Fund Missions. They Fund Organizations. Make Sure Yours Is Ready.

Funders understand this. The most sophisticated foundations and government grant programs specifically look for evidence of strong organizational capacity before committing significant resources to an organization. They want to know that their investment will be managed well, that programs will be implemented effectively, that outcomes will be measured and reported accurately, and that the organization has the internal strength to sustain the work beyond the grant period.

Rachel Bennett leads GRANTEdge's capacity building work, helping organizations diagnose their current capacity levels, prioritize the improvements that will have the greatest impact on their funding competitiveness and operational effectiveness, and implement those improvements in a structured, supported, and sustainable way.

Our capacity building support covers governance and board development, financial management and accounting practices, human resources and personnel systems, program design and evaluation frameworks, technology and data management, communications and knowledge management, strategic planning, and the overall management infrastructure that determines organizational effectiveness.

Strong organizations win more funding. It is that simple. When your internal systems are professional, your compliance is solid, your board is engaged, and your outcome measurement is rigorous, every proposal you submit is stronger, every funder relationship is more credible, and every funding opportunity is more accessible.

Donor Engagement Strategy

Your Donors Are Your Most Renewable Funding Source. Are You Treating Them That Way?

Individual donors represent one of the most valuable and most underutilized funding resources available to most nonprofits. Unlike grants, which are time-limited, competitive, and subject to shifting funder priorities, individual donors when properly cultivated and stewarded can become loyal, long-term partners who grow their giving year after year and become some of your organization's most powerful advocates.

Yet most nonprofits treat their donors transactionally. They make the ask. They receive the gift. They send a thank-you letter. And then they wait until the next campaign to make contact again. This approach produces weak retention rates, stagnant donor bases, and missed major gift opportunities.

GRANTEdge develops structured, relationship-centered donor engagement strategies that transform the way organizations communicate with, cultivate, and retain their donor communities.

Working with our development expertise and digital visibility knowledge, we design donor engagement systems that create meaningful, consistent touchpoints throughout the year, segment your donor base to ensure communications are relevant and personalized, build stewardship programs that recognize and celebrate donor loyalty, develop major gifts pipelines that identify and cultivate your highest-potential supporters, and integrate digital channels including email, social media, and your website into a cohesive donor communication ecosystem.

Donor retention is one of the highest-return investments a nonprofit can make. Acquiring a new donor costs significantly more than retaining an existing one. Yet the average nonprofit donor retention rate hovers below fifty percent. GRANTEdge helps you build the systems and the culture that dramatically improve those numbers.

Google Ad Grants Setup, Review, and Management

Google Is Offering Your Nonprofit Up to Ten Thousand Dollars Per Month in Free Advertising. Are You Using It?

The Google Ad Grants program is one of the most powerful and most underutilized resources available to eligible nonprofit organizations. Google provides qualifying 501(c)(3) organizations with up to ten thousand dollars per month in free advertising on the Google Search Network, allowing them to appear at the top of search results for terms directly related to their mission, their programs, their services, and their funding opportunities.

For organizations that claim this grant and manage it effectively, the results are significant and measurable. More qualified visitors to your website. More awareness among the specific audiences most likely to support your mission. More volunteer inquiries. More donor engagement. More program participants. And stronger digital credibility in the eyes of funders who research your organization online.

For organizations that do not claim this grant, or that claim it and fail to manage it in compliance with Google's strict program requirements, the opportunity is simply lost. Many organizations fall into exactly this category, either unaware of the program, overwhelmed by its technical requirements, or managing it without the expertise to get meaningful results.

Basit Ola leads GRANTEdge's Google Ad Grants practice, bringing comprehensive expertise in every aspect of the program from initial eligibility verification and application support through account setup, campaign strategy, keyword research, ad copy development, compliance management, and ongoing performance optimization.

Program Eligibility

To be eligible for Google Ad Grants, your organization must hold valid 501(c)(3) status, be registered with Google for Nonprofits, comply with Google's program policies, and maintain a high-quality website that meets Google's standards. We help you verify your eligibility, complete the application process, and ensure your website meets program requirements before account activation.

Account Setup and Structure

A properly structured Google Ad Grants account is the foundation of strong performance. We build your account with the strategic structure, campaign organization, keyword architecture, and ad group design that positions your campaigns for maximum effectiveness from the start.

Keyword Strategy

The keywords you target determine who sees your ads and how relevant that audience is to your mission. We conduct comprehensive keyword research to identify the specific search terms your target audiences including funders, donors, volunteers, and program participants are using to find organizations like yours.

Compliance Management

Google Ad Grants accounts are subject to strict compliance requirements including minimum click-through rate thresholds, quality score standards, keyword type restrictions, and ad relevance requirements. Accounts that fall out of compliance are suspended, often without warning. We monitor your account continuously to ensure it remains fully compliant.

Ongoing Optimization

Google Ad Grants performance improves over time when accounts are actively and expertly managed. We conduct regular optimization reviews, adjusting keyword bids, adding new keyword opportunities, pausing underperforming elements, testing new ad variations, and refining landing page alignment.

Performance Reporting

We provide regular performance reports that translate Google Ads metrics into the terms that matter most to your organization, including website visits driven, audience segments reached, conversion actions completed, and the overall value delivered by your Ad Grants investment.

  • Full account setup with strategic campaign and ad group structure
  • Comprehensive keyword research and strategy development
  • Ad copy writing, testing, and ongoing refinement
  • Compliance monitoring and proactive management to prevent suspension
  • Ongoing campaign optimization and performance improvement
  • Regular performance reporting with mission-relevant metrics
  • Strategic recommendations for integrating Ad Grants performance with your broader visibility and funding strategy
