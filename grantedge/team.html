The Professionals Powering Your Funding Success

Leadership and Expertise Driving Sustainable Funding

At GRANTEdge, your mission is supported by seasoned professionals who bring strategic depth, specialized knowledge, and coordinated execution to every dimension of nonprofit funding and organizational growth.

When You Partner With GRANTEdge, You Work With Specialists Who Have Dedicated Their Professional Expertise to One Purpose: Helping Mission-Driven Organizations Build Sustainable Funding.

Sustainable funding is not built through isolated effort. It is built through disciplined strategy, intelligent research, compelling communication, strong organizational infrastructure, and credible digital presence working in alignment. The GRANTEdge leadership brings together expertise across each of these critical domains, operating not as disconnected consultants but as a coordinated leadership team committed to advancing mission-driven organizations with integrity, clarity, and measurable impact.

Basit Ola

Digital Visibility and Brand Positioning Expert

Basit Ola brings strategic expertise in digital visibility, brand positioning, and online authority development to the GRANTEdge team and to every organization we serve.

In the contemporary funding landscape, a nonprofit's digital presence is inseparable from its credibility and its competitive position. Before program officers review proposals, they visit websites. Before major donors make gifts, they search for your organization online. What they find in those moments either reinforces the case for investing in your mission or quietly undermines it.

Basit's work ensures that every organization GRANTEdge serves passes that test with distinction. He specializes in nonprofit brand strategy and positioning, helping organizations clarify and communicate their identity, their impact, and their value proposition in ways that resonate with funders, donors, and community partners across every digital and communications channel.

His expertise in Google Ad Grants management is particularly valuable. He manages the full Google Ad Grants lifecycle for GRANTEdge clients, from eligibility verification and program application through account setup, campaign strategy, keyword research, ad copy development, compliance monitoring, and continuous performance optimization.

Core Areas of Expertise

  • Digital brand strategy and nonprofit positioning
  • Google Ad Grants setup, compliance management, and performance optimization
  • Digital visibility auditing and improvement strategy
  • Search presence development and optimization
  • Social media strategy and content planning
  • Online presence management for funding credibility
  • Digital communications integration with funding strategy

Harriet Edward

Grant Strategy and Research Specialist

Harriet Edward brings exceptional depth and analytical rigor to GRANTEdge's grant strategy and research practice, serving as the intelligence engine that ensures every organization we work with is pursuing the right funders for the right reasons with the right strategic approach.

Harriet's research methodology is systematic, data-informed, and thorough. She examines foundation IRS filings and public giving records to understand actual giving patterns, not just stated priorities. She analyzes funded portfolios to understand what types of organizations, programs, and communities each funder actually supports.

She builds detailed prospect profiles that give GRANTEdge's proposal leadership and each client's organizational leadership the intelligence they need to approach each funder with confidence, to tailor proposals precisely to funder priorities, and to make informed decisions about how to prioritize application efforts.

Core Areas of Expertise

  • Systematic funder research and prospect qualification
  • Foundation, government, and corporate giving landscape analysis
  • Funder prospect profile development and intelligence reporting
  • Grant pipeline development and multi-year strategy planning
  • Funding opportunity monitoring and emerging opportunity identification
  • Funder alignment analysis and application prioritization strategy

Rachel Bennett

Nonprofit Development and Compliance Consultant

Rachel Bennett serves as GRANTEdge's nonprofit development and compliance specialist, bringing deep expertise in organizational readiness, development strategy, compliance standards, and the internal infrastructure that determines whether a nonprofit can compete for and successfully manage significant funding.

Rachel's expertise addresses one of the most consistently overlooked dimensions of the funding challenge: that winning grants requires not just compelling proposals and strategic funder research, but an organizational infrastructure that funders can trust.

Rachel works with organizations at every stage of development to assess their current organizational health honestly and comprehensively, identify the specific gaps most likely to concern funders or create operational risk, prioritize improvements in the sequence that delivers the greatest impact, and support implementation of those improvements in a practical, realistic, and sustainable way.

Core Areas of Expertise

  • Comprehensive grant readiness and organizational readiness assessment
  • Nonprofit compliance standards review and gap analysis
  • Development strategy development and implementation planning
  • Board fundraising governance and engagement strategy
  • Program evaluation framework and outcome measurement development
  • Financial management practices review and improvement guidance
  • Capacity building strategy and implementation support

Kevin George

Senior Grant Proposal and Funding Communications Specialist

Kevin George leads GRANTEdge's grant proposal development and funding communications practice, bringing the experience, the strategic insight, and the writing excellence that transforms mission-driven work into funded programs.

Kevin has mastered this challenge across a wide range of funder types, funding levels, and program areas. He approaches every proposal as a unique strategic communication challenge, beginning with a thorough review of funder guidelines, evaluation criteria, and giving priorities before developing the specific narrative strategy that will be most effective.

Beyond individual grant proposals, Kevin develops the full suite of funding communications that support a comprehensive development operation including letters of inquiry, case statements, impact reports, donor communications, and funding communications strategies.

Core Areas of Expertise

  • Full grant proposal development across all funder types and funding sizes
  • Letters of inquiry and pre-application communications
  • Case for support and organizational narrative development
  • Federal, state, foundation, and corporate grant applications
  • Grant reporting and funder stewardship communications
  • Impact report and annual report content development
  • Funding communications strategy and organizational messaging
