01

Small and Grassroots Organizations

Your Organization May Be Small. Your Mission Is Not. Let Us Help You Fund It Like It Matters.

Small and grassroots organizations occupy a unique and vital position in the nonprofit ecosystem. They are often the closest to the communities they serve, the most trusted by the people they support, and the most innovative in their approaches to entrenched challenges. They are also frequently the most under-resourced when it comes to development capacity, funding infrastructure, and organizational systems.

GRANTEdge works with small and grassroots organizations to address this paradox directly. We begin where you are. We assess your current readiness level honestly and without judgment. We identify the specific gaps between where you are and where you need to be to compete for the funding your mission deserves.

What We Do for Small and Grassroots Organizations

  • Grant readiness assessment and gap-closing roadmap
  • Funder research focused on foundations that support grassroots and early-stage organizations
  • Grant proposal development that positions emerging organizations compellingly
  • Foundational fundraising strategy development
  • Basic organizational capacity building support
  • Google Ad Grants setup and management to build digital visibility cost-effectively
